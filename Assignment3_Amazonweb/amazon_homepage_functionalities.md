# Amazon Homepage Functionalities

- Search products across departments using the main search box and category dropdown.
- Change delivery location through the Deliver to control.
- Sign in or manage account settings from Account & Lists.
- Review past purchases through Returns & Orders.
- Open the shopping cart and check item count.
- Switch language or shopping country/region.
- Open the main category menu for broader navigation.
- Browse featured promotions in the hero carousel.
- Jump into high-level shopping areas like Today's Deals, Prime Video, Gift Cards, Sell, Customer Service, and Registry.
- Explore curated product collections and deal tiles from the homepage modules.
- Open footer links for help, policies, account tools, and Amazon ecosystem services.
- Navigate back to the top quickly from the footer.
